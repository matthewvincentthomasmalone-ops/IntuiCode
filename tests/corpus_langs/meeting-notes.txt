Allotment committee meeting, 3 November

Present: Sam, Jo, Priya, Tom and Alice. Apologies from Ken.

The water troughs on the north side are leaking again. Tom will ask the
council whether the repair can be done before the frosts, and if not we
will turn the supply off at the stopcock for the winter.

Plot 14 has been empty since the summer. Two people on the waiting list
have asked about it. Priya will write to both and offer a half plot each,
since neither of them wants a full one.

The seed swap will be on the second Saturday in February in the hut.
Alice is collecting packets in the meantime. Please label anything you
saved yourself with the variety and the year.

Money: the account stands at a little over four hundred pounds after the
insurance payment. Rents for next year stay the same.

Any other business: someone has been leaving the gate open at night.
Please lock it behind you, even in daylight if nobody else is about.

Next meeting: first Monday in December, seven o'clock, in the hut.

Actions

- Tom: ask the council about the troughs.
- Priya: write to the two people on the waiting list.
- Alice: collect and sort seed packets before February.
- Everyone: lock the gate.
